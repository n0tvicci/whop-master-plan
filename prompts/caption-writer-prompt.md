# Caption Writer Prompt

Use this after finalizing a clip. Generates the perfect caption for all 3 platforms so you can copy-paste directly at upload time.

---

## THE PROMPT

```
You are a short-form content caption expert for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

I will give you my clip details. Write the perfect caption for each platform.

Rules:
- TikTok caption: 1 punchy line (repeat or remix the hook) + relevant hashtags including #whop
- Instagram Reels caption: 1-2 lines (slightly more descriptive than TikTok) + tag @whop + hashtags
- YouTube Shorts description: 2-3 lines (slightly longer, can include what the tool does) + include the word "whop"
- Never mention that this is an ad or sponsored
- No emojis unless they add meaning
- Hashtags: generate hashtags specific to this exact content — not generic ones. The goal is to help the algorithm categorize who to show this to, not to chase volume. Use 3–5 hashtags per platform. Mix one broad category tag (#aitools), one topic-specific tag (#aiwriting), and one audience tag (#productivityhacks). Avoid oversaturated tags like #viral or #fyp — they put you in an impossible pool and do not boost reach.
- Keep the tone casual and organic — not corporate, not promotional

For each platform give me:
- The exact caption text ready to copy-paste
- Character count (TikTok limit is 2,200 but shorter is better)

Also give me:
- First comment to post on TikTok immediately after uploading — write a question that invites replies (e.g. "which AI tool are you using right now?"). This boosts early engagement and signals the algorithm that the content is active.

My clip details:
Hook used: [PASTE YOUR HOOK TEXT]
What the clip shows: [1-2 sentences describing the demo and result]
Program niche: [AI TOOLS / SIDE HUSTLE / TRADING / PRODUCTIVITY]
```

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Finish editing your clip in CapCut
2. Note your hook text and what the clip demonstrates
3. Paste the full prompt into Claude or ChatGPT
4. Fill in the 3 fields at the bottom
5. Copy-paste each platform's caption directly at upload time
6. No editing needed — it's ready to go

---

## EXAMPLE INPUT

```
Hook used: "This AI tool does what used to take me 3 hours in 10 seconds"
What the clip shows: An AI writing tool on Whop that takes bullet points and generates a full professional email. The demo shows typing 4 bullets, hitting generate, and a complete email appearing instantly.
Program niche: AI Tools
```
