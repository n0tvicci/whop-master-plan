# Weekly Review Prompt

Use this every Sunday. Paste your week's stats and get a specific action plan for the next 7 days.

---

## THE PROMPT

```
You are a short-form content strategist specializing in Whop clipping.

I will give you my weekly posting stats. Analyze what worked and what didn't, then give me a specific action plan for next week.

Analyze:
1. Best performing clip — what made the hook, demo, or audio work? Was the completion rate high?
2. Worst performing clip — what specifically failed (hook, length, audio, niche)? Was it low views or low completion?
3. Best platform this week — which one gave the most views and why?
4. Saves and shares analysis — which clip got the most saves? Most shares? These matter more than raw views for algorithm growth.
5. Best posting day — which day of the week performed best? Any pattern?
6. Pattern recognition — is there a pattern in your top 3 clips? (same hook style, same audio energy, same topic?)

Then give me next week's action plan:
- Hook format to use most next week (based on what worked)
- Hook format to stop using (based on what failed)
- Audio energy to target (based on best performing clip)
- Topic to focus on (based on pattern)
- Best day and time to post next week (based on which day/time performed best this week)
- One specific experiment to try — give a clear hypothesis: "Test X on Y day because Z"
- Realistic view target for next week based on this week's numbers

My stats this week:

Total clips posted: [NUMBER]
Total views across all platforms: [NUMBER]

Clip breakdown (list each clip):
- Clip 1: [HOOK TEXT] | [TOPIC] | [VIEWS] | [SAVES] | [SHARES] | [AVG WATCH TIME or COMPLETION %] | [DAY POSTED] | [PLATFORM THAT DID BEST]
- Clip 2: [HOOK TEXT] | [TOPIC] | [VIEWS] | [SAVES] | [SHARES] | [AVG WATCH TIME or COMPLETION %] | [DAY POSTED] | [PLATFORM THAT DID BEST]
- Clip 3: [HOOK TEXT] | [TOPIC] | [VIEWS] | [SAVES] | [SHARES] | [AVG WATCH TIME or COMPLETION %] | [DAY POSTED] | [PLATFORM THAT DID BEST]
(add more rows as needed — write "unknown" for any stat you can't find)

Current month earnings from Whop: $[AMOUNT]
```

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Every Sunday, check your TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube analytics
2. Copy the view count for each clip you posted that week
3. Paste the full prompt into Claude or ChatGPT
4. Fill in all the stats
5. Follow the action plan for the next 7 days exactly
6. Repeat every Sunday
