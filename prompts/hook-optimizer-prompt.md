# Hook Optimizer Prompt

Use this when a clip underperforms. Paste your original hook and the AI will generate stronger alternatives to test.

---

## THE PROMPT

```
You are a short-form video hook expert specializing in faceless content for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

A clip I posted underperformed. I will give you the original hook and some context.

First, diagnose why the original hook failed. Choose the most likely reason:
- Too generic — could apply to any tool, no specificity
- Too vague — doesn't tell the viewer what they're about to see
- Too long — hook text exceeded 8 words, lost people before they read it
- No visual contrast — the text blended into the background
- Weak opening word — started with a weak word like "this" or "I" instead of a pattern-interrupting word
- No curiosity gap — told the viewer everything upfront, nothing to stay for
- Sounds like an ad — felt promotional, not like genuine discovery

Then rewrite it into 5 stronger variations.

Rules:
- No voiceover — hooks are bold text on screen only
- Hook must land in under 3 seconds
- No mention of "Whop" anywhere
- Should feel like organic discovery content, not an ad
- Each variation must use a different hook format:
    Variation 1 → Curiosity gap ("This [thing] does what...")
    Variation 2 → POV ("POV: You just found...")
    Variation 3 → Controversy ("Everyone is sleeping on...")
    Variation 4 → Number/Stat ("This tool saves [X] hours...")
    Variation 5 → Pain point ("Stop [doing wrong thing]...")

For each variation give me:
- The exact hook text (what appears on screen — maximum 8 words)
- Text formatting: recommended font size (large/extra large), text color, and screen position (top/center/bottom)
- One sentence on why this angle is stronger than the original
- Audio sync note: at what moment in the audio should this text appear for maximum impact?
- Difficulty to execute: Easy / Medium (does it require a different demo setup?)

Original hook: [PASTE YOUR ORIGINAL HOOK TEXT HERE]
What the clip was about: [DESCRIBE THE PROGRAM OR TOOL IN 1-2 SENTENCES]
Total views: [PASTE VIEW COUNT]
Average completion rate (if available): [e.g. 15% / unknown]
Platform it flopped on: [TIKTOK / REELS / SHORTS / ALL THREE]
```

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Identify a clip that got significantly fewer views than your average
2. Copy the original hook text and clip context
3. Paste the full prompt into Claude or ChatGPT
4. Fill in all 4 fields at the bottom
5. Pick the strongest variation and re-make the clip with the new hook
6. Keep the same demo and result — only change the hook

---

## EXAMPLE INPUT

```
Original hook: "This AI tool is actually really useful"
What the clip was about: An AI tool that rewrites any email to sound more professional in 5 seconds
How many views it got: 180 views
Platform it flopped on: All three
```
