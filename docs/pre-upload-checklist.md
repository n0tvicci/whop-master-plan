# Pre-Upload Viral Checklist

Run through this before uploading every single clip. If any item is unchecked, fix it first.

---

## HOOK (First 3 Seconds)

- [ ] Bold text appears on screen within the first second
- [ ] The hook creates curiosity, shock, or a "wait what" reaction
- [ ] No slow intro — clip starts immediately with energy
- [ ] Audio drops right at the first frame, not after
- [ ] You would stop scrolling for this hook if you saw it on your feed

---

## DEMO (Seconds 3–18)

- [ ] Screen recording is clear and high quality — not blurry or laggy
- [ ] There is zero dead air — something is always happening on screen
- [ ] Cuts happen every 1–2 seconds — nothing overstays its welcome
- [ ] Text overlays are present on screen explaining each step
- [ ] Text is short — 1 to 5 words per frame, not full sentences
- [ ] Text tone is informal and reactive ("wait what" / "no way" / "bro what" / "I can't believe this")
- [ ] Zoom punch or effect used at least once on a key moment

---

## RESULT (Seconds 18–28)

- [ ] The final output or outcome is clearly visible on screen
- [ ] A reaction text overlay is on the result frame
- [ ] Clip ends before it drags — cut it the moment the result is shown

---

## AUDIO

- [ ] Audio is from TikTok's built-in sound library — not an external file
- [ ] The sound is currently trending on TikTok
- [ ] Audio energy matches the clip (hype beat for reveals, satisfying build for demos)
- [ ] Volume is balanced — audio is audible but not overpowering the visuals

---

## TECHNICAL

- [ ] Clip length is between 20 and 28 seconds
- [ ] Video is vertical — 9:16 ratio
- [ ] Resolution is 1080p or higher — no pixelation
- [ ] No TikTok watermark from another account's video
- [ ] Whop is NOT mentioned anywhere in the video

---

## CAPTION

- [ ] First line of caption repeats the hook or asks a question
- [ ] Hashtags included: `#aitools #sidehustle #productivity #whop`
- [ ] Caption is short — 1 line + hashtags only, nothing else

---

## PLATFORM SETTINGS

- [ ] **TikTok** — `#whop` added to caption for Content Rewards tracking
- [ ] **Instagram Reels** — `@whop` tagged in the caption
- [ ] **YouTube Shorts** — "whop" included somewhere in the description
- [ ] Posting time is 7–10am Philippine time (= 7–10pm EST, US prime time)

---

## FINAL VIBE CHECK

- [ ] Does this feel like organic discovery content — not an ad?
- [ ] Would a stranger stop scrolling for this?
- [ ] Is the hook the strongest it can possibly be?

---

**If all boxes are checked — upload it.**  
**If even one box is unchecked — fix it first, then upload.**
