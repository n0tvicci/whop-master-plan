## What is Whop Clipping?

Whop is a platform where creators sell digital programs — AI tools, communities, courses, side hustle systems. Whop runs a **Content Rewards program** that pays you per 1,000 views when you create short-form video clips showcasing those programs.

You create original clips → post on TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts → Whop tracks views → you get paid.

**You earn through views, not referrals. No affiliate links. No selling.**

---

## Step Zero: Sign Up for Whop Content Rewards

**Before anything else — you must register as a creator on Whop or you will never get paid regardless of how many views you get.**

1. Go to `whop.com` and create a free account
2. Navigate to the **Clips** or **Creator** section (whop.com/clips)
3. Apply to become a content creator — fill in your social accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)
4. Wait for approval (usually fast, sometimes instant)
5. Once approved, browse available campaigns and pick programs to clip
6. Each campaign will show: payout rate, content requirements, geo-restrictions, and budget

**Do not skip this step.** Posting without being registered means Whop cannot track your views and you earn nothing.

## Account Setup

**New accounts, not aged — always for beginners.**  
Aged accounts cost $20–$200+ and are often farmed, flagged, or shadowbanned. TikTok gives new accounts a discovery boost in the first 2–4 weeks to test content — use that window. The algorithm is content-first, not account-age-first.

Use the same name across all 3 platforms.

**Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts  
**Content style:** 100% faceless — no face, no voiceover

**Profile setup (all free):**
- Profile pic: clean abstract logo made in Canva free tier
- Bio: *"Daily clips. Real tools. Real results."*
- No referral links in bio — income comes from views, not clicks

**Account warmup (do this before your first post — applies to all 3 platforms):**

- Day 1–2: Complete your profile, scroll your niche for 15–20 min/day, like 10–15 videos, follow 5–10 accounts, leave 2–3 comments. Do NOT post yet.
- Day 3: Post your first clip — just 1, not 3
- Days 4–7: Gradually increase to 2 then 3 clips/day

> TikTok is the strictest about warmup — new accounts that jump straight to 3+ posts/day get flagged fast. Instagram and YouTube Shorts are more lenient but warming up still helps the algorithm categorize your account correctly.

**Shadowban — what to avoid:**

- **No VPN** *(All 3 platforms)* — TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube all flag unusual IP switching as bot behavior
- **No reposting clips that have a TikTok watermark** *(All 3 platforms)* — TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube actively suppress watermarked content from other platforms
- **No posting 5+ videos on day 1** *(All 3 platforms)* — all three platforms treat first-day posting floods as spam signals
- **No copy-pasting the same caption repeatedly** *(All 3 platforms)* — flagged as automated behavior across all platforms
- **No posting then immediately deleting** *(All 3 platforms)* — signals low-quality farming on all three
- **No buying followers or engagement** *(All 3 platforms)* — instant flag risk on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube
- **No music outside the platform's licensed library** *(Platform-specific)*
  - TikTok: use TikTok's built-in Sounds library only
  - Instagram Reels: use Instagram's Music library only
  - YouTube Shorts: use YouTube's Audio Library or copyright-free tracks only

## The Clip Formula

Every clip follows this exact 3-beat structure. No exceptions.

```
[0–3s]   HOOK    — Stop the scroll with bold text + audio drop
[3–18s]  DEMO    — Screen recording of the tool with text overlays
[18–28s] RESULT  — Show the final output or outcome
```

**Total length:** 20–28 seconds  
**No voiceover needed.** The story is told through:
- Bold text overlays (1–5 words per frame)
- Screen recording of the tool in action
- Trending viral audio underneath

**Hook examples:**
1. `"This AI tool does what used to take me 3 hours..."`
2. `"Nobody is talking about this and I don't know why"`
3. `"POV: You find the most underrated AI tool online"`
4. `"I tested every AI program I could find — this one actually works"`
5. `"This just replaced [expensive tool] for free"`

**Text overlay style:** Informal and reactive  
Examples: *"wait what"* | *"no way this is free"* | *"I've been doing it wrong"* | *"bro what"*

**Never mention Whop in the video.** It should feel like organic discovery content, not an ad.

## How to Find Programs to Clip

1. Go to `whop.com` and browse the Creator/Clips dashboard
2. Look for active campaigns with visible budgets
3. Pick programs that have:
   - High budget (more money = higher payout per 1k views)
   - Visual product you can screen record in action
   - Clear demonstrable result within 20 seconds
   - Active community (signals the program is real)
4. Avoid programs that are text-heavy courses with nothing visual to show

**What you screen record:**
- The program's dashboard opening
- The AI tool running and producing an output
- The results, features, or outputs in real time

**How to access programs you haven't paid for:**
Most Whop programs require payment to access the full dashboard. Here is how to get content without paying:
- **Free trials** — many Whop programs offer a free trial. Sign up, screen record what you need, cancel before billing.
- **Free tier programs** — filter for programs that have a free tier on Whop. These give you full dashboard access.
- **Program landing pages** — the Whop program's public sales page often has demo videos, screenshots, and feature previews you can clip.
- **Creator briefs** — when you're approved for a campaign, Whop often provides content guidelines and sample assets you can use.
- **Prioritize free-access programs first** — in the beginning, only clip programs you can actually access so your screen recordings are real and credible.

## Tier 1 Country Strategy

Many campaigns only pay for views from Tier 1 countries: **US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand.**

To attract a Tier 1 audience even while posting from the Philippines:

- **Post only in English** — no local references ever
- **Use US-trending audio** — TikTok pushes content to audiences already engaging with that sound
- **Post at US prime time** — 7–10pm EST = 7–10am Philippine time
- **Pick US-dominant topics** — AI tools and income content is overwhelmingly consumed by US audiences
- **Do NOT use a VPN to fake your region** — TikTok flags this and it causes the shadowban you're trying to avoid. Let the content naturally attract US viewers instead.
- **When browsing campaigns:** prioritize ones with no geo-restriction as a safety net while your audience builds

## Posting Schedule

**Daily workflow (fits inside 2–4 hours):**

**Step 1 — Morning (8–9am PH) | 30 min**
Browse Whop creator dashboard. Pick 1–2 programs to clip. Plan what you'll screen record for each.

**Step 2 — Afternoon | 90 min**
Screen record the programs. Edit 3 clips in CapCut. Add text overlays, trending audio, zoom effects.

**Step 3 — Evening (7–8am PH = 7–8pm EST) | 30–45 min**
Upload the same clip to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Write captions + hashtags for each.

**Caption formula:**
- Line 1: Repeat the hook or ask a question
- Hashtags: `#aitools #sidehustle #productivity #whop`
- Keep it short — 1 line + hashtags

**Same clip, all 3 platforms — but export correctly.**

**Critical workflow — follow this order every time:**
1. Finish editing in CapCut
2. Export from CapCut — **remove the CapCut watermark before exporting** (CapCut has a free "remove watermark" button in the export screen)
3. Save the clean video file to your phone/PC
4. Upload that same clean file separately to TikTok, then Reels, then Shorts
5. Never download a video from TikTok and repost it to other platforms — the TikTok watermark will get penalized on Instagram and YouTube

**Per-platform caption adjustments:**
- TikTok: Add `#whop` in caption for Content Rewards tracking
- Instagram Reels: Tag `@whop` in caption — limit to 2 Reels per day maximum (posting 3+ hurts reach on Instagram)
- YouTube Shorts: Include "whop" somewhere in the description

## $500/Month Roadmap

**Month 1 — Learn the system. Post daily. Don't quit.**
Views target: 0–20k | Estimated earnings: $0 (expect nothing in Month 1 — this is your foundation month)

**Month 2 — Double down on what worked. Improve hooks.**
Views target: 20k–60k | Estimated earnings: $80–$240

**Month 3 — Optimize. Study your best clips. Improve cuts.**
Views target: 60k–120k | Estimated earnings: $240–$480

**Month 4 — Consistency. Scale winners. First $500 month.**
Views target: 120k–150k+ | Estimated earnings: $480–$600

**Month 1 mindset:** Nothing will go viral immediately. The goal is to build the habit and learn what the algorithm rewards. Every clip is a data point.

**How to find trending audio:**
- Open TikTok → tap the search icon → go to Sounds → Trending
- When scrolling your feed, if a sound is used in multiple viral videos in the same day, save it immediately
- Use that sound within 24–48 hours while it is still rising — not after it peaks
- On Instagram, trending audio shows a small arrow icon next to the sound name when creating a Reel

**Weekly optimization — what to actually do:**
Every week, look at your last 7 clips and answer these 4 questions:

1. Which clip got the most views? — Recreate a version of it with a different hook next week
2. What was the hook on your worst-performing clip? — That hook is dead, never use that format again
3. What audio was used on your best clip? — Use the same audio or similar energy audio on next week's clips
4. Which platform gave you the most views? — Post your best clip there first next week

The rule: **if a clip formula worked once, make 3 more versions of it before moving on.**
