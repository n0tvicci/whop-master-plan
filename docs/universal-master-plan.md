## Step Zero: Whop Creator Registration

**Do this before anything else or you will never get paid.**

1. Create a free account at `whop.com`
2. Navigate to `whop.com/clips` — the Content Rewards creator dashboard
3. Apply as a creator — submit your TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube handles
4. Wait for approval (usually fast, sometimes same-day)
5. Once approved: browse active campaigns, check payout rates, geo-restrictions, and campaign budgets
6. Confirm which platforms count toward payout for each campaign you join

**Do not post until you are approved.** Views before approval earn nothing.

## Account Setup

**Use new accounts, not aged.** New accounts get a discovery window in the first 2–4 weeks. Use it.

- Same username across all 3 platforms — clean and memorable
- Profile pic: clean abstract logo (Canva free tier)
- Bio: one line, no affiliate links — income comes from views only
- Content style: 100% faceless, no face, no voiceover

**Account Warmup (do not skip on any platform):**
- Day 1–2: Complete profile, scroll your niche 15–20 min/day, like 10–15 videos, follow 5–10 accounts, leave 2–3 comments. Do NOT post.
- Day 3: Post 1 clip only
- Days 4–7: Ramp to 2 then 3 clips/day

**What kills new accounts fast:**
- VPN use on any platform
- Reposting content with a TikTok watermark to Instagram or YouTube
- Posting 5+ videos on Day 1
- Copy-pasting identical captions across every post
- Posting then immediately deleting
- Buying followers or engagement
- Using unlicensed music — use each platform's built-in audio library only

**One niche per account. No mixing for the first 60 days.** The algorithm needs to categorize your account — switching topics confuses it and tanks distribution.

**Dedicated pages:** Some campaigns require you to name your account after the creator you're clipping — a dedicated page. Check the campaign requirements before setting up your account name for that campaign.

**Multiple accounts = multiple income streams.** Each account is an asset. More accounts means more posting slots, more campaigns you can run simultaneously, and more views. Start with one, then scale to multiple accounts across niches once you have the system working.

## Campaign Selection

**Before recording anything, run every campaign through:**
`prompts/program-evaluator-prompt.md`

Only proceed if verdict is **CLIP IT** or **CLIP IT IF** (and you meet the condition). Skip anything below **18/30**.

**Also check before recording:**
- Does the campaign have remaining budget? No budget = no payout even with views.
- Is the campaign geo-restricted? Tier 1 only, or global?
- Can you access the program without paying? Free trial, free tier, or public demo page.
- What is the payout rate? Rates vary widely — some campaigns pay $0.50/1k (music, entertainment), others pay $3–5/1k (AI tools, finance). Factor this into your effort-to-earnings calculation.

**Two ways to get your footage — know which applies:**

- **Self-sourced:** You find the footage yourself — free trial access, the program's landing page trailer, the creator's public social posts, or public member highlights. You find the best moment and build the clip around it.
- **Provided content:** The campaign gives you source videos (podcast footage, vlogs, community highlights, music videos). Read the campaign's Google Doc — it tells you exactly what source material they're providing and what they want clipped.

Either way: identify the single most surprising, funny, or curiosity-triggering 20–28 second moment in the source material, then build your clip around that moment.

## The Clip Formula

Every clip follows this 3-beat structure. No exceptions.

```
[0–3s]   HOOK      — Bold text on screen. One idea. Must stop the scroll.
[3–18s]  SHOWCASE  — Your footage. Cut every 1–2 seconds. Community highlights, exclusive content previews, reactions.
[18–28s] PAYOFF    — The most compelling or FOMO-triggering moment. Reaction text overlay.
```

**Total length:** 20–28 seconds. No voiceover. Story is told through text overlays + your footage + trending audio.

**Inside the Demo — what keeps people watching:**
- **Seconds 3–8:** Add a retention hook — tease the result before showing it. "and this is where it gets insane" as a text overlay.
- **Seconds 10–12:** Add a pattern interrupt — a sudden zoom, color shift in text, or a bold new overlay. Re-grabs anyone who started drifting.
- **Result design:** Make the final output surprising or satisfying enough that someone watches again. Loops count as views and signal the algorithm to push harder.

**Text overlay rules:**
- 1–5 words per frame, never full sentences
- Informal and reactive: *"wait what" | "no way" | "bro what" | "I can't believe this"*
- Cut every 1–2 seconds — never let a frame overstay

**Never mention Whop in the video.** Must feel like organic discovery content, not a promotion.

## Posting Schedule

**Morning | 8–9am PH | 30 min**
Browse Whop. Evaluate campaigns with `program-evaluator-prompt.md`. Lock in 1–2 programs to clip today.

**Midday | 1–3pm PH | 90 min**
Generate ideas with `content-idea-prompt.md`. Capture your footage. Edit 3 clips in CapCut. Run `pre-upload-checklist.md`. Export without watermark.

**Evening | 7–8pm PH | 30–45 min**
Write captions with `caption-writer-prompt.md`. Schedule uploads to TikTok → Reels → Shorts for 7–9am PH delivery using each platform's built-in scheduler. Set an alarm — when TikTok goes live at 7–9am PH, post the first comment within 60 seconds. **Submit each clip link to Whop after it goes live** (see Submission section below).

**Saturday — Batch Day:**
Make 5–7 clips, save as drafts. Never let the draft folder drop below 3. Pull from drafts on bad days — never skip posting. **Plan for 5–8 hours — this is a full day of work.**

**Posting time:** 7–9am PH = 7–9pm EDT (US prime time). Do not post live in the evening — 7–8pm PH = 6–7am EST, which is US early morning, not prime time. Always use scheduled posting.

## Submitting Your Clip to Whop

**Posting alone does not get you paid. You must submit the clip link to Whop after every upload.**

After your clip is live on TikTok, Reels, or Shorts:
1. Copy the video link
2. Go to the campaign page on Whop
3. Submit the link — enter the title and paste the URL
4. Whop reviews it, approves it, and tracks the views
5. Once approved and views accumulate, payout processes automatically

Do this for every clip, every platform, every day. Skipping the submission means the views count for nothing.

## Handling Rejections

If a clip gets rejected, do not repost or give up — read the rejection reason first.

**Most common rejection reason — analytics:**
1. Go to the campaign's support chat on Whop
2. Send a full screen recording of your analytics for that video
3. Whop will review and manually approve if the views are legitimate

Other rejection reasons (wrong format, wrong content, missing requirements) are listed in the rejection notice — fix the specific issue and resubmit.

## Tier 1 Audience Strategy

Most high-payout campaigns only count views from **US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand.**

- Post only in English — no local references
- Use US-trending audio — TikTok pushes content to audiences already engaging with that sound
- Post at US prime time
- Never use a VPN to fake your location — causes shadowban

**Track Tier 1 ratio weekly:**
TikTok Analytics → Followers → Top territories. If US + UK + CA + AU + NZ combined is under 40%, do not scale volume yet — adjust post time and audio first.

**Finding trending audio:**
TikTok → Search → Sounds → Trending. If a sound appears in multiple viral clips the same day, save it and use it within 24–48 hours while it's still rising.

## Weekly Review

Every Sunday, use:
`prompts/weekly-review-prompt.md`

Paste your week's stats and follow the action plan it returns for the next 7 days exactly.

**5 questions to answer every week:**
1. Which clip got the most views? Make 3 more versions with different hooks.
2. What was the hook on the worst clip? That format is dead — retire it.
3. What audio was on the best clip? Use that same energy next week.
4. Which platform gave the most views? Post your best clip there first next week.
5. What is your Tier 1 ratio? If under 40%, fix timing and audio before scaling volume.

**Rule: If a clip formula worked once, make 3 more versions before moving on.**

## $500/Month Roadmap

**Payout assumption:** These projections assume campaigns paying $2.50–$4/1k views (AI tools, SaaS, finance, trading). Entertainment campaigns pay $0.50–$1/1k — at those rates, $500/month requires 500k–1M views and is not realistic in this timeframe. Prioritize high-payout campaigns.

**Month 1**
Views: 0–20k | Earnings: $0
Post every day. Learn what the algorithm rewards. Every clip is a data point.

**Month 2**
Views: 20k–60k | Earnings: $60–$240
Double down on what worked. Improve hooks.

**Month 3**
Views: 60k–120k | Earnings: $240–$480
Optimize weekly. Fix underperformers. Study your best clips.

**Month 4–6**
Views: 120k–167k+ | Earnings: $480–$600+
Consistency. Scale winners. First $500 month.

**Month 1 pivot signal:**
If by Day 30 your best clip has under 1,000 views — stop. Rewrite every hook format. Do not scale until at least one clip breaks 1,000 views.
