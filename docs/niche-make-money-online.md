# Niche: Make Money Online / Side Hustles

**Goal:** $500/month through Whop Content Rewards  
**Strategy:** Faceless, no voiceover, text + audio, screen recording only  
**Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts

---

## Why This Niche Works

Income proof content has the highest stopping power on all 3 platforms. People freeze when they see money screenshots, earnings dashboards, or "I made $X doing this" content. Whop has a massive library of side hustle programs to clip from and the audience is overwhelmingly US-based — natural Tier 1 traffic with no extra effort.

---

## What You Screen Record

- Earnings dashboards and income screenshots from inside Whop programs
- "What's inside this program" walkthroughs
- Step-by-step breakdowns of the side hustle method
- Results and proof shared inside the community

---

## Clip Formula

```
[0–3s]   HOOK    — Income proof screenshot or bold earnings claim
[3–18s]  DEMO    — Screen record the program, show the method or dashboard
[18–28s] RESULT  — Show the earnings result or final outcome
```

**Total length:** 20–28 seconds  
**No voiceover.** Text overlays tell the story. Trending audio carries the energy.

Upload the same clip to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts with captions and hashtags.
