# Niche: Trading and Finance

**Goal:** $500/month through Whop Content Rewards  
**Strategy:** Faceless, no voiceover, text + audio, screen recording only  
**Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts

---

## Why This Niche Works

Trading and finance content has one of the highest payout rates per 1k views on all platforms — advertisers pay more to reach this audience, which often means higher Whop campaign budgets too. The audience skews heavily US and UK, making Tier 1 views almost guaranteed if the content is in English. Charts, dashboards, and profit screenshots are naturally visual and very easy to screen record with no face or voice needed.

---

## What You Screen Record

- Trading dashboards and charts moving in real time
- Profit screenshots and account growth from inside Whop programs
- Strategy breakdowns shown on a chart
- "What this Whop trading community teaches" walkthroughs

---

## Clip Formula

```
[0–3s]   HOOK    — Profit screenshot or chart with bold result text
[3–18s]  DEMO    — Screen record the strategy or program walkthrough
[18–28s] RESULT  — Show the trade outcome or earnings result
```

**Total length:** 20–28 seconds  
**No voiceover.** Text overlays tell the story. Trending audio carries the energy.

---

## Hook Examples

1. `"This trading strategy turned $500 into $2,000 in one week"`
2. `"The trading community nobody is talking about"`
3. `"I tested 10 trading programs — this one actually works"`
4. `"POV: You find a trading program that explains it like you're 5"`
5. `"This chart pattern prints money and most traders ignore it"`

## Account Warmup

Same rules as the main plan:
- Day 1–2: Complete profile, scroll trading/finance content, like and follow — do not post

## Tier 1 Strategy

- Post only in English
- Use US-trending audio
- Post at 7–10am Philippine time (= 7–10pm EST)
- Set TikTok account region to US on creation
- Prioritize Whop campaigns with no geo-restriction

---

## Posting Schedule

**Step 1 — Morning (8–9am PH) | 30 min**
Browse Whop for trading and finance programs. Pick 1–2 with clear chart or earnings proof to clip.

**Step 2 — Afternoon | 90 min**
Screen record the strategy, chart, or program walkthrough. Edit 3 clips in CapCut with text overlays and trending audio.

**Step 3 — Evening (7–8am PH = 7–8pm EST) | 30–45 min**
Upload the same clip to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts with captions and hashtags.

---

## Caption Formula

- Line 1: Repeat the hook or ask a question
- Hashtags: `#trading #stockmarket #forex #passiveincome #whop`
- TikTok: add `#whop` for Content Rewards tracking
- Instagram: tag `@whop`
- YouTube: include "whop" in description
- Always add: `not financial advice` somewhere in the caption
